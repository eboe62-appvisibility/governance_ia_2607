## Purpose

---

## Description

Provide a clear, factual description of the knowledge asset.

Focus on stable knowledge rather than temporary observations or interpretations.

---

## Key Characteristics

List the most relevant attributes that define this knowledge asset.

Examples:

- identifiers
- names
- classifications
- properties
- dates
- locations
- technical characteristics
- linguistic features

Include only characteristics supported by available evidence.

---

## Scope

Describe what this knowledge asset covers.

Also describe what is explicitly outside its scope.

---

## Evidence and Sources

List the sources supporting this knowledge asset.

Examples:

- project documentation
- official publications
- research sources
- standards
- reference works
- verified datasets

Clearly distinguish:

- verified evidence
- assumptions
- unresolved questions

## Validation

## Notes

Additional information useful for future maintenance or extension.

---

## Prohibited Actions

Do not:

- document temporary information as stable knowledge
- mix verified facts with assumptions
- duplicate existing Knowledge Assets without justification
- introduce unsupported conclusions
- modify related Knowledge Assets without explicit review
